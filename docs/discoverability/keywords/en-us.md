## Notes for the reviewer

- The **on-screen label is "Quote" but the printed title is "ESTIMATE"** (§D).
  Both are search terms and both belong in the listing.
- "Bill of lading" is a different legal document — include it only if the
  reviewer confirms people search it *expecting* a packing slip. Otherwise cut
  it: a term that brings the wrong searcher converts badly and helps nothing.
- Sales tax is per-state and user-configured. The listing must not imply DocFlow
  knows a rate (§W — nothing here is tax advice).
