## Notes for the reviewer

- **This locale most likely needs splitting.** *Presupuesto* dominates in Spain,
  *cotización* in Latin America; *albarán* is Spain, *guía de remisión* is
  MX/PE and is a fiscal document there. One ES sheet probably cannot serve both
  — say so if that is the case, and the terminology table splits with it.
- Cover the unaccented spellings people type (`cotizacion`, `guia`).
- *Guía de remisión* carries fiscal meaning in some markets. The listing must
  not imply DocFlow produces a compliant fiscal document (§W).
