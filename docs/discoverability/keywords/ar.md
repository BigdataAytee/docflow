## Notes for the reviewer

- Arabic document vocabulary varies sharply by market (Gulf vs Levant vs North
  Africa). Say which market this sheet is for, and whether it can serve others.
- Confirm which delivery-document term a customer actually uses — the table
  drafts *بوليصة شحن* from §D, but *إذن تسليم* may be the everyday word.
- Confirm the store fields index Arabic without normalisation surprises
  (hamza, taa marbuta, and whether diacritics affect matching).
- Mixed-direction strings in the listing (an Arabic title containing "PDF" or
  "DocFlow") need checking for how the store actually renders them.
